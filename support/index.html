Support

Something not working, or a question about Roundup? Email apps@yushes.com and we’ll get back to you.

How do I start a game?

Open a group chat with at least 3 people in Messages. Tap the apps button next to the text field, pick Roundup, and tap Start Roundup in this chat. Choose a prompt and tap Send to chat.

How do my friends join?

They tap the Roundup bubble in the chat. If they don’t have Roundup yet, Messages offers to get it from the App Store.

Where is the Roundup icon?

Roundup lives only in Messages, so it has no icon on your Home Screen. Find it in the apps row in any chat.

Someone already started a game in our chat

Tap their Roundup bubble to join. Each chat plays one game.

When does each round end?

Answers close Sunday at 8pm. Then everyone who answered guesses who said what, and the reveal follows.

How do I report or block someone?

Tap the … next to any answer to report it or block the person who wrote it. You can also block players from Roundup’s Settings, and unblock them there. We review reports within 24 hours.

How do I delete my account?

In Roundup, open Settings and tap Delete my account. See the privacy policy for what that removes.
