Your group chat’s weekly game

One prompt a week, right in iMessage. Everyone answers in secret, then you guess who said what.

How it works

Pick a promptEach week one person picks a prompt, or writes their own, and sends it to the chat.
Answer in secretEveryone answers privately. Nobody sees names yet.
Guess who said itGuess who wrote each answer, then see the reveal and the standings.

Two games

Who Said It: answer the prompt, then guess who wrote each answer.
Superlatives: vote on who’s most likely to… and see who the chat picks.

Get it

Roundup lives in Messages. Get it from the App Store, then open any group chat of 3 or more people, tap the apps button, and pick Roundup. Friends join by tapping the Roundup bubble.
